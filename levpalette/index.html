Copiar, exportar e compartilhar Ao copiar, o HEX vai para a área de transferência Android. Ao exportar, o PNG vai para o destino escolhido. LeVPalette — Privacy policy LeVSoft · Version 0.3.0 · November 9, 2026 Images and local processing You choose an image through Android's picker. Extraction of up to five colors takes place on your device. LeVPalette does not send images or palettes to LeVSoft servers or store the original photo. It requires no account and includes no ads or analytics SDKs. Palettes and preferences Palettes are stored in the app's private database with an identifier, name, creation date and HEX codes. You can delete them in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that database. Your language preference is kept in Android settings or local app storage depending on your Android version. Automatic app data backup is disabled. Copy, export and share Copying puts the HEX code on Android's clipboard. Exporting writes a PNG to your chosen destination. Sharing gives your chosen app temporary read access to the PNG artwork without the original photo. Android may clear the cache; when preparing a PNG for export or sharing, LeVPalette attempts to delete its own temporary files last modified more than seven days ago. This cleanup is not scheduled and does not affect previously exported PNGs. External services and deletion Cloud image/file providers and recipient apps may use a network and handle files under their own policies. Manage exported PNGs and shared copies at their destination; LeVPalette does not control those copies or synchronization. Local palettes remain until you delete them or remove the app's data.
